The badge moves to the top-left, and the bar finds your cursor.

## What's new

- **The badge is now in the top-left corner.** `i_rdy`, `i_thnk`, `i_fin` and `i_err` work the same as in 0.4.3, just on the other side of the screen.
- **The bar sits next to your text cursor.** If you use the bar instead of the badge (Settings → General), it now appears just below where you're typing, follows along as you type, and moves above the line when you're near the bottom of the screen. Before, it often ended up in the middle at the bottom of the window, because many apps (Windows 11 Notepad, Windows Terminal, browsers) don't report their cursor the old way. When Inkling can't find the cursor at all, the bar goes next to the mouse pointer.
- The README now tells the story of how Inkling came about.

## For developers

**Badge** (`Ui/StatusOverlay.cs`)
- `BadgeIndicator.Start` anchors to `WorkingArea.Left + margin` instead of the right edge. The Settings label, tutorial text, README and `ReadmeMedia` (GIFs re-rendered) follow.

**Caret lookup** (new `Ui/CaretLocator.cs`)
- Three methods, in order:
  1. The Win32 caret (`GetGUIThreadInfo`, as before). This is cheap and runs on the UI thread.
  2. MSAA: `AccessibleObjectFromWindow(hwndFocus, OBJID_CARET)` then `accLocation`. This covers Chromium and most Win32 controls.
  3. UI Automation: `AutomationElement.FocusedElement`, `TextPattern.GetSelection()`. An empty (caret) range has no rectangle in most providers, so it measures the character after the caret, or the one before it at the end of the text.
- Methods 2 and 3 are cross-process calls, and `BarIndicator.Display` runs inside the keyboard hook. So they run on a pool thread with a 300 ms limit (`FindAsync`), and the result is applied with `BeginInvoke`. While a lookup runs, a bar that's already showing stays where it was, and a new one waits for the answer. A `_wanted` flag stops a late answer from reopening a bar that was hidden in the meantime.
- Results that look like "no caret" are dropped: zero height, (0, 0), or off every screen.
- `StatusOverlay.AnchorPoint` now takes the caret rectangle. It places the bar 6 px below the caret, or above it when there's no room, or near the mouse pointer when there's no caret. The old bottom-centre-of-window fallback is gone.
- UI Automation's client side is warmed on a background thread at startup (`CaretLocator.Warm`), since its first call is slow.

**Dev flag**
- `--caret-test [seconds]` prints, once a second, what each method finds in the foreground window and how long that took. It sends no input.
